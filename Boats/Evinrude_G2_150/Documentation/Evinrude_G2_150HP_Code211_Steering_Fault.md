## Symptom

Diagnostic display showing **Code 211** — a power steering fault.

## What Code 211 actually is

Per G2 owner forums (Barnacle Bill's Marine Supply / etecownersgroup.com), code 211 is an **open circuit fault in the Dynamic Power Steering (DPS) system**. Commonly traced to:

- A bad connection at the DPS motor plug
- The DPS ground lead to the block
- The DPS power lead to the positive battery stud

Some owners report the code appearing temporarily and clearing on its own; others report a persistent loss of power steering assist. Comprehensive diagnosis would use EvDiag6 diagnostic software (dealer-level tool) or the service manual.

**This reads as a common electrical-connection issue reported by other owners, not a confirmed match to the mechanical/hydraulic recalls below** — none of the official recall notices mention diagnostic code numbers. Worth checking both angles.

## Active recalls that match this outboard's specs (2017, 150HP, G2)

### 1. Gearcase and exhaust housing extension nuts (likely does NOT apply — build date mismatch)

- **Announced**: August 17, 2017
- **Affected**: E-TEC G2 150–300 HP, **built June or July 2017**
- **Issue**: nuts on the gearcase and exhaust housing extension may loosen, allowing the gearcase assembly to detach from the outboard — can cause an abrupt directional change and loss of steering
- **Fix**: free dealer service, ~90 minutes
- **This engine (built 02/2017) falls outside the affected build window** — confirmed not a likely match, but still worth a quick check with BRP using serial 05488084 since exact serial ranges aren't published

### 2. Loss of hydraulic steering fluid

- **Issue**: two T45 pipe plugs on the outboard's stern and swivel assembly were not sufficiently tightened at the factory and can loosen, leaking hydraulic steering fluid and causing loss of steering control
- **Affected range**: not specified in the public notice ("Evinrude E-TEC outboard equipped with a steering system") — worth checking regardless of exact build date

### 3. Incorrectly assembled steering component

- **Announced**: June 18, 2015
- **Affected**: E-TEC G2 74° V6 (3.4L) — the same engine block used in the 150–200HP G2 range
- **Issue**: a limited number of outboards may have a steering component incorrectly assembled at the factory, risking loss of steering control
- **Note**: predates a 2017 build — only relevant if the recall's serial range extends further than its 2015 announcement date suggests (unconfirmed)

## What to do

**Call BRP/Evinrude directly with your outboard's serial number** — this is the only way to get definitive recall status, since exact serial ranges aren't published:

**1-844-345-4277** (Monday–Friday, 9:30am–4:30pm CST)

Recommended approach:
1. Call and check recall eligibility against all three recalls above using your serial number.
2. Regardless of recall status, check the DPS motor plug connection, the DPS ground lead to the block, and the DPS power lead to the battery stud — the most commonly reported fix for code 211 specifically among other owners.
3. If recall-eligible, schedule the free dealer service — if not, the DPS connection troubleshooting above is the next step, or a dealer diagnostic session with EvDiag6 if the simple connection checks don't resolve it.
