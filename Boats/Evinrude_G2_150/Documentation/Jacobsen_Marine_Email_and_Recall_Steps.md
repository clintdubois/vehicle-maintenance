## Steps to confirm recall status

Do this before or alongside contacting Jacobsen's — recall repairs are free, so it's worth knowing status before paying for a diagnostic visit that might be covered.

1. **Call BRP directly**: 1-844-345-4277 (Monday–Friday, 9:30am–4:30pm CST).
2. **Have ready when you call**:
   - Model: **C150FXHAAD**
   - Serial number: **05488084**
   - Build date: **02/2017**
3. **Ask specifically** whether serial 05488084 is covered under any of these three recalls:
   - Gearcase and exhaust housing extension nuts (built June/July 2017 — your Feb 2017 build likely falls outside this window, but confirm directly since exact serial ranges aren't published)
   - Loss of hydraulic steering fluid (T45 pipe plugs on stern/swivel assembly)
   - Incorrectly assembled steering component (2015-announced, G2 74° V6 3.4L)
4. **If recall-eligible**: ask BRP whether the repair should be done through Jacobsen's Marine or another authorized dealer near you, and whether it needs to happen before or can happen alongside the Code 211 diagnostic visit.
5. **If not recall-eligible**: proceed with the Jacobsen's Marine visit as a standard (paid) diagnostic.
6. **Either way**, mention the Code 211 fault and ask BRP if it's a symptom they'd expect from any of the three recalls, or if it sounds unrelated (electrical DPS connection issue instead) — their answer can help you brief Jacobsen's more precisely when you call/email them.
